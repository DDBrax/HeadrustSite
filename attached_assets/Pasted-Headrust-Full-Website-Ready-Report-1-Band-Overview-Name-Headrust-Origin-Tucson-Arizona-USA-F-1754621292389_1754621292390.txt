Extended Bio (for About page or press kits)

Since rising from Tucson’s underground scene in 2005, Headrust has refined their heavy-metal sound with unrelenting vigor. Fronted by Dennis Brack, with driving riffs from Steve Urquides and thunderous drumming by George Samaniego, the trio has carved a growing reputation on the local stage.
